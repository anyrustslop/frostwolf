1 Your search engine

Your searches say a lot about you. FrostWolf searches with DuckDuckGo, without AI by default and has removed Google, Bing, Amazon and eBay. These private search engines are already installed:

DuckDuckGo (no AI)

Default

No search history, no profile of you, and no AI: no AI answers, no Duck.ai, AI-generated images filtered out.

Shortcut: just type in the address bar, or @ddg

Brave Search

Its own independent index (not Google or Bing), no tracking, no profiling.

Shortcut: @brave your search

Mojeek

A small UK engine with its own crawler and a strict no-tracking policy since 2004.

Shortcut: @mojeek your search

Qwant

A European search engine under EU privacy law; no ad profiling from your searches.

Shortcut: @qwant your search

Change your default: menu ☰ › Settings › Search › Default search engine. Or type @brave, @mojeek or @qwant before a search to use one just once.

3 Should you use a VPN?

What a VPN does

  • Hides your IP address (and so your rough location) from the websites you visit.
  • Hides which sites you visit from your internet provider and public Wi-Fi.
  • Lets you avoid local blocking and snooping on hotel, café and school networks.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't make you anonymous: logging in, cookies and fingerprints still identify you (FrostWolf handles those).
  • It moves trust from your provider to the VPN company, so pick one that's been independently audited.
  • Avoid free, unknown VPNs: many sell your data, which defeats the point.

Good to know

  • Stay logged in after closing: Settings › Privacy › uncheck Delete cookies and site data when FrostWolf is closed (or add exceptions for your favourite sites).
  • Netflix, Spotify Web and other DRM: Settings › General › Play DRM-controlled content.
  • A site doesn't work? Click the uBlock Origin icon and turn it off for that site, or click the shield in the address bar.
